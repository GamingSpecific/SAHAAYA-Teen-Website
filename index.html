About us

SAHAAYA is a teen-led mental health initiative built on a simple belief: sometimes, people don't need someone to fix everything. They just need a safe place to start.

As teenagers, we understand how difficult it can be to talk about what we're going through. Academic pressure, friendships, family expectations, loneliness, social media, and simply trying to figure yourself out can sometimes feel like a lot. Yet asking for help can feel even harder.

SAHAAYA was created to make that first step a little easier.

We aim to provide accessible, age-appropriate information, practical tools, conversation starters, and pathways to reliable professional support, all in one safe and welcoming space.

We don't want to speak for teenagers. We want to create something with them.

SAHAAYA is not a replacement for professional mental-health care, and we don't ask you to share your personal story with us. Our goal is simple: help you understand, help you find the words, and help you find the right support.
